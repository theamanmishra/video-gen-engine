Research checked 2026-11-13. This is a source-linked handoff for story refinement. It does not establish actual Concord incidents, installed OEMs, validated SOPs or measured improvements. The local sources are this version's story.md, the saved competitor playbook and compiler context. The selected scenario and shared numbers are defined in execution brief.md. Only the accepted v01 factory geometry is an agreed visual direction.

## Recommendation for the wider treatment

Keep the tipped handled-jug incident as the long, recognizable episode. Add brief machine-history rows for an empty-jug supply gap, product waiting and downstream blocking. Use a diminishing empty-jug queue for an early-warning example where response lead time can be made assessable. Use the lay-down contrast for process understanding. Keep the wider incident catalogue below available to the execution agent, without making the film sixteen separate incident stories.

The ending should follow the same incident category through a recorded expert cue, an owned follow-up and later comparable-run review. A short recovery today is not proof that the underlying cause has been removed. Another operator should retrieve the saved cue and its conditions. A manager should choose an improvement and be able to check its outcome.

The best new direct analogy is Septimatech's fallen-container case. It also reminds us that observation can justify a practical mechanical improvement. The camera or chatbot does not have to be the physical remedy.

## Mechanisms to investigate behind the incident labels

This separates a larger incident library from a false list of proven root causes. The observed event, physical mechanism, contributing condition and systemic driver are different claims. A camera may show the first and useful cues for the others. The sources above establish that these are relevant engineering questions, not that they explain Concord's actual losses. The primary E-014 guide finding is explicitly authored for the concept film.

| Candidate mechanism | Linked scenario | Evidence needed before confirming it | Film use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transfer-guide arrangement does not match the container-format reference | I01/I02 | Relevant reference, a qualified inspection and source footage showing the entry symptom | Chosen illustrative reviewed finding for E-014. No actual clearance or adjustment value |
| Transfer support or alignment creates an unstable entry | I01/I02 | Inspection of the actual transfer, comparable format and corroborating footage | Alternative hypothesis; do not infer a hidden support mismatch from pose alone |
| Excess accumulation pressure or contact destabilizes the jug | I01/I02 | Visible contact/spacing sequence plus transfer and conveyor context | Alternative hypothesis if the footage depicts it, not a universal guide fault |

| Container shape or stability makes the handling design unsuitable | I01/I11 | Actual package/handling assessment. Septimatech's slender-container case supplies a different-container analogy | Research reference only; do not assign its small-base mechanism to broad handled jugs |
| Empty-container replenishment does not reach the infeed in time | I03 | Monitored supply gap, replenishment workflow and responsible-role context | Chosen early-warning illustration. Identify the practical action instead of blaming the operator |
| An unavailable batch or interruption on the active product route delays syrup supply | I05 | Cooking/batch status, actual active route, holding or flow context and operator confirmation | History category. Closed stainless vessels are not transparent to an overview camera |
| Filling setup or a valve/nozzle condition contributes to dripping | I10 | Actual filler design, inspection, relevant setup history and matched observation | Alternative physical episode; equipment-specific OEM controls are not transferable instructions |
| Closure supply or feeding is interrupted | I04/I12 | Installed closure system, exposed feed evidence and relevant machine/inspection context | Reserve until the hardware is confirmed |
| Case/blank shortage, deformation or feeding difficulty initiates a packing stop | I06/I07/I08 | Actual case-packing arrangement, visible supply/pick evidence and material or mechanism inspection | Candidate downstream detail. Default E-018 keeps its exact mechanism under review |
| A closure/adhesive process or program change alters box formation | I09/I06 | Confirmation the machine uses that process, maintenance/program history and qualified testing | Raven supplies the investigation structure. Do not show its bag/box PLC finding as Concord's |
| Restart restores motion while the relevant operating/quality condition remains unresolved | I13 | Product/mode reference, observed flow and appropriate independent quality/release evidence | Carry the running-well distinction through the recovery narrative |

A systemic issue, such as an unclear format reference or a missing verification record, may help explain recurrence. It must not be inferred solely because the visible event repeated. State the missing evidence or use a clearly authored demonstration record. An action that changes a symptom once is not proof of the deeper mechanism or a lasting fix.

## Process-specific evidence sources

The local process source brief establishes a recorded lay-down stage before cooling, not a confirmed treatment specification. [Australis](https://australiseng.com.au/product/lay-down-conveyors/) explains intentionally changing container orientation, including HDPE. [Sentry](https://www.sentryequipment.com/products/laydown-sterilizer/) describes a post-capping/pre-cooling hot-fill implementation. These support the contrast between wrong orientation before filling and expected orientation in an appropriate stage. Do not copy generic angles, times or temperatures.

[Filamatic's setup guidance](https://www.filamatic.com/blog/correct-liquid-filling-machine-set-up-procedures-to-improve-efficiency/) supports the general relevance of filling setup to dripping and foaming. Its [in-case-filler brochure](https://www.filamatic.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/In-Case-Filler.pdf), pages 2 and 3, includes fill-weight and closure-torque checks. Neither source identifies Concord's machine or establishes a startup SOP.

[Siemens' PackML documentation](https://cache.industry.siemens.com/dl/files/198/109821198/att_1143917/v1/LPMLV2022_SIMATIC_V1_0_en.pdf), printed page 49, distinguishes missing upstream input from inability to discharge downstream. Use starved and blocked from the named machine's perspective. If the filler stops, its downstream neighbour can become starved, but a downstream queue blocking the filler is not downstream starvation of the filler.

## Practical choice for a production-ready story brief

Use I01 as the main incident, with the expert cue focused on the observed entry condition and a separately identified illustrative inspection finding, as specified in execution brief.md. Use I03 and I06 to make the machine history and management question broader than one jam. Carry I13 into recovery so a green running signal is not the only endpoint. Use I14 as an illustrative process-context distinction with its site qualification. The default procedural moment uses a plainly labelled demonstration restart-verification record. An actual site SOP is needed before claiming customer noncompliance.

The operator answer should open the selected clip, relevant expert cue, applicability and approved procedure. The management answer should distinguish recurring local losses from waiting imposed by another stage, then propose an owner and a comparable follow-up. It should not add all stopped machine minutes into plant loss.

The knowledge record retains the observable cue, source episode, applicable jug format and infeed arrangement, reviewed response, inspection source, unresolved questions and review date. The authored E-014 physical finding and its fictional status are defined in execution brief.md. Repeated attempts are useful evidence to inspect but do not automatically prove a lasting fix. A later state should show both use by another shift and what happened after the chosen improvement.
