# Real footage search, 2026-11-14

Aman has no footage of Concord's line. He asked for a search for public filling-line videos we could use. Nothing has been downloaded.

## Answer

No free clip shows a half-gallon handled syrup jug line, and none shows a jug tipping. The best free, commercially usable find is one Pexels series of a **sauce bottling line** with an operator in PPE. It fits the camera-feed shots. YouTube has many OEM videos of handled-gallon jug lines, but they are useful only as modelling reference.

Contacting uploaders is outreach and is not authorized without Aman.

## Review flags

- Only the 10416676 page and thumbnail were viewed. No clip was played end to end.
- YouTube licences are unconfirmed. Check each video's description for "Creative Commons Attribution" before any use, and credit the uploader.
- Real footage of a foreign sauce plant must not be presented as Concord's line. Keep the "Illustrative" tag on camera shots.
