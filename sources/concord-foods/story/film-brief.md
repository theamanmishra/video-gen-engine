The local infeed-obstruction family is **3 episodes and 150 seconds**. Empty-jug supply is **70 seconds**. Propagated downstream waiting is **120 seconds**. Those three filler categories sum to 340 seconds. The 180-second origin interval belongs to case packing and must not be added to the filler total.

The local family's average interruption duration is **50 seconds**, calculated as 150/3. It is not mean repair time, mean operator response or an estimate of every future incident. One E-014 episode has a duration, not a mean. Avoid additional aggregates unless they are computed from this ledger.

| Element | Required behaviour |
|---|---|

| Rates | Use observed container-flow units for discrete stages. | Interaction | Cursor lands on the machine, click opens history, episode selection opens its detail, source control opens the right replay. Scrub or jump through selected points on the common clock instead of playing a 54-second episode in full |
| Knowledge | Show the source action beside the editable cue, review/correction and applicability. Change shift or user while retaining the same record |
| Management answer | Become a readable analysis and selected task. The result is not only a long chat paragraph |
| Proactive alert | Show observed depletion, acknowledgement, human response and outcome. Do not claim guaranteed avoidance or camera-to-machine control |
| Procedure prompt | Identify the expected step and evidence/record gap. Use a demo checklist until an actual site SOP is available. Prefer review language over blame |
| Trust drawer | Keep accepted inputs, rule/version, result, interpretation and human source distinct. Repeatability is not correctness |

One visual hierarchy should carry the film. Physical evidence first, short explanatory labels second, detail revealed by interaction. The selected asset, event and clock survive transitions.
